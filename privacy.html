Privacy

Privacy notice.

How the draft website handles inquiries and browsing.

Contact inquiries

The website contact form uses a mailto link to prepare an email in your own email application. It does not submit messages to a website database. Your email provider handles the message once you send it.

Preferences

The appearance setting stores Day, Night or System in your browser’s local storage. The website does not use an analytics tracker by default.

Programme privacy

Do not submit medical histories, identity documents or sensitive beneficiary data using public email. A separate secure process is needed before collecting beneficiary applications.
