Founded in Gombe State, the Adamu Yola Foundation makes more opportunities available to young people and communities through education, skills, health and sport.

Our Story

Local roots. Practical possibility.

The foundation began with a simple conviction: young people from Gombe South deserve the same opportunities enjoyed by young people anywhere else. That idea connects everything we do, from educational assistance and vocational learning to grassroots sport and community health.

Our sporting story started first. The Gombe South Unity Cup kicked off in 2016, and its success in bringing communities together laid the ground for the foundation, established in 2018 and headquartered in Gombe. In earlier reporting and on our previous website, the organisation appears as the Adamu Yola Ture Foundation.

Today the foundation works under the banner of sports, education and entrepreneurship, guided by a motto our supporters know well: charity begins at home.

Mission, Vision & Values

What we work towards.

Our mission

To open pathways to opportunity for young people and communities in Gombe through education, practical skills, community health and grassroots sport.

Our vision

A Gombe South where every young person has the same chance to learn, earn, stay healthy and shine as young people anywhere.

Respect

For the people we serve, for opponents on the pitch and for every community we work in.

Opportunity

We look for talent and potential, then remove the barriers in front of them.

Accountability

We document our work with dates, places, partners and real outcomes.

Unity

Peaceful coexistence and fair play guide everything, from tournaments to outreach.

Leadership & History

How the foundation is run.

The foundation is registered as Incorporated Trustees and governed by its Board of Trustees, who set direction and safeguard how resources are used.

Board of Trustees

Provides oversight, approves programmes and protects the foundation’s integrity.

Unity Cup Organising Committee

Plans each edition: club registration, fixtures, venues, officiating and media.

Volunteers and partners

Coaches, officials, media partners such as Amana FM and allies such as the Jordan Nwora Foundation.
