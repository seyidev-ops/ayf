Who we are

Adamu Yola Foundation is a Gombe-based nonprofit with a focus on enabling young people and strengthening communities. Public organisational records give 2018 as its founding year. Earlier sports activities associated with its work began in Gombe South in 2016.

Historically also referred to as Adamu Yola Ture Foundation, the organisation is known for community football and youth sports development and has identified education, vocational skills and medical outreach among its core programmes.

Our direction is simple: create more pathways to learning, participation and self-reliance while making room for partners and communities to work together.
