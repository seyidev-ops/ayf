About Us

A foundation built around people.

Founded in Gombe State, the Adamu Yola Foundation works to make more opportunities available to young people and communities through education, skills, sports and community initiatives.

Who we are

Local roots. Practical possibility.

The foundation’s public description speaks of ensuring young people from Gombe South have the same opportunities enjoyed by young people elsewhere. That idea connects its programmes: educational assistance, vocational learning, grassroots sports, and community-facing support.

AYF is publicly described as a nonprofit headquartered in Gombe, founded in 2018. Its Unity Cup history reaches back to 2016 — showing an established sporting initiative from which later work has grown.

The historic name Adamu Yola Ture Foundation appears in earlier reporting and the old website; its official present legal naming remains subject to administrative verification.

Documented foundations

Community: Gombe South and the wider Gombe State.

Public focus: youth opportunity, education, empowerment and unity.

Charity begins at home: the foundation’s public LinkedIn tagline.

Legal documentation: an AYF letterhead lists IT/NO 117968. The current legal registration record should be verified.

Source: official LinkedIn listing, historical AYF website and project-provided documentation.

How the work developed

Not just one programme.

01 / Education & opportunity

Support for learners

The foundation identifies scholarships and education assistance as areas for helping indigent students at secondary and tertiary levels.

02 / Livelihoods

Practical empowerment

Skills training joins earlier community initiatives, including a publicly described agricultural-support programme in Ture Ward, where a distribution plan involving 200 fertiliser bags was documented.

03 / Peace & sport

Football beyond the scoreline

The Unity Cup's earlier website articulated a Discover–Develop–Promote approach and a commitment to respect, fair play and peaceful coexistence.

04 / Health & community

Access and participation

Medical outreach and practical community support form part of the foundation’s stated programme scope. Individual event records are required before beneficiary totals or treatment claims are published.

Our approach

Respect. Opportunity. Accountability.

Rather than relying on unsourced claims, AYF's website should document initiatives with dates, places, organisers, real images and outcomes. The public website is built to grow as verified records arrive.

Additional historic programmes

The former AYF website listed the North-East Elite Basketball Camp, fertiliser distribution, a qualitative learning-environment initiative and widows’ assistance as historical event categories.

These remain in the archive while their dates, permissions and original photographs are recovered. They are not advertised as currently open programmes.
