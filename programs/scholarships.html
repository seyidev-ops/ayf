Educational Empowerment

Helping students continue their education when financial barriers threaten their progress.

Programme enquiries →

Illustrative image; not an AYF event photograph.

What this programme represents

Why it matters.

Educational opportunity should not depend solely on a family’s ability to meet every schooling expense. This programme area focuses on financial assistance and scholarships for indigent learners at secondary and tertiary levels.

Programme approach

How the work takes shape.

01

Schooling access

Support with eligible education-related costs for students experiencing financial hardship.

02

Secondary and tertiary education

An approach that recognises needs at both school and higher-education levels.

03

Responsible selection

Clear application, eligibility, verification and selection processes should be published whenever opportunities open.

04

Learning progress

Longer-term support depends on verified programme records and collaboration with families and learning institutions.
