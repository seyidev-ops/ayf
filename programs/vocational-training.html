What this programme represents

Why it matters.

Vocational training responds to the gap between ambition and usable skills. Through hands-on learning, participants can build technical capabilities for work, small enterprise and everyday economic independence.

Programme status

Individual trades, cohorts, training centres, dates and participation requirements await official programme confirmation.

Programme approach

How the work takes shape.

01

Skills assessment

Identify community needs and the trades or practical competencies participants want to develop.

02

Hands-on learning

Training should be oriented around practice, guided demonstration and finished work.

03

Enterprise readiness

Where provided, connect training to basic customer service, costing, record-keeping and employability.

04

Evidence of progress

Document completed sessions, participant work and follow-on opportunities rather than promising instant income.
