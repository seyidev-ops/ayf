Gombe South Unity Cup

A long-running grassroots football platform bringing communities together across Gombe South while encouraging sporting excellence and peaceful coexistence.

Enquire about the Unity Cup ↗
Why it matters

More than football. A meeting place for peace, talent and community.

Our approach is about connecting possibility with practical action, in ways that respond to real community needs.

FROM THE ARCHIVE

Documented tournament history

Public reporting records the ninth edition during the 2025–2026 season, as well as earlier peacebuilding and community outreach efforts connected with the competition.
