Gombe South Unity Cup

Promoting peace and discovering football talent across the communities of Gombe South. This is the competition’s own emblem, displayed separately from match and archive photography.

What this programme represents

Why it matters.

The Unity Cup began in 2016 and has developed into a recurring football initiative across Gombe South. The foundation’s earlier website describes a model that discovers young talent through competition, develops players through follow-up camps and engagement, and seeks opportunities to promote them to a broader sporting audience.

Programme status

The next Unity Cup edition is being prepared. The opening date, registration details, venues and regulations should be announced by the organising committee; no new dates are invented here.

Programme approach

How the work takes shape.

01

Discover

Community teams and grassroots players compete, giving emerging talent a stage to be noticed.

02

Develop

Documented past editions have included post-tournament camping, scouting opportunities and training engagement.

03

Promote

The foundation has described efforts to connect outstanding players with clubs and wider pathways.

04

Unite

Respect, fair play, peaceful coexistence and drug-abuse prevention have accompanied the on-field competition.
