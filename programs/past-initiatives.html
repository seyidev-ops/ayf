Past initiatives & collaborations.

Beyond our five current programmes, the foundation has supported athletes, farmers, learners and families, often alongside trusted partners.

InitiativesCollaborations

Initiatives

What we have done.

2021

North-East Elite Basketball Camp

A development camp that gathered young basketball talent from across the North-East, delivered in collaboration with the Jordan Nwora Foundation.

Source: Sports247

Community initiative

Ture Ward agricultural empowerment

Farmers in Ture Ward, Kaltungo West, received support through the distribution of 200 bags of fertiliser.

Source: Adamu Yola Foundation

2022

Player camp in Abuja

Eighteen players shortlisted from the sixth Unity Cup were invited to a closed camp in Abuja, a pathway from community football to wider opportunity.

Source: Sports247

2020

SportVote partnership

A partnership around the 5th Unity Cup to raise the visibility of grassroots players through sports technology.

Source: Futbaloven

2023

Amana FM broadcast partnership

Amana FM became the official broadcast partner of the 8th Unity Cup, taking match coverage to radio listeners.

Source: Adamu Yola Foundation

Across Unity Cup editions

Peace and drug-abuse prevention campaigns

Peace campaigns and anti-drug messages, including the Say No To Drugs call, have run alongside the tournament.

Source: Instagram @adamuyolafdn

Historic programme

Better learning environments

An initiative focused on improving the quality of learning environments for students.

Historic programme

Widows’ assistance

Practical support for widows in our communities, part of the foundation’s commitment that charity begins at home.
