Our work, explained.

Five programme areas reflect the foundation’s focus on opportunity, learning, health, community participation and grassroots talent.

Educational ScholarshipsVocational TrainingGombe South Unity CupMedical OutreachPrincipals' CupPast initiatives

Education

Educational Scholarships

Educational opportunity should not depend on a family’s ability to meet every schooling expense. This programme provides financial assistance and scholarships for indigent learners at secondary and tertiary levels, so that talent and effort decide how far a student goes.

Read about Educational Scholarships

Skills

Vocational Training

Vocational training closes the gap between ambition and usable skills. Through hands-on learning, participants build technical abilities for work, small enterprise and everyday economic independence, in line with the foundation’s focus on education, entrepreneurship and youth development.

Read about Vocational Training

Grassroots sport

Gombe South Unity Cup

The Unity Cup began in 2016 and has grown into the foundation’s best-known initiative, played across Gombe South. Its model is simple: discover young talent through competition, develop players through camps and engagement, and promote them to a wider sporting audience, all while using football to promote peace between communities.

Read about Gombe South Unity Cup

Health

Medical Outreach

For many households, even basic check-ups and preventive care are hard to reach. Medical outreach connects vulnerable communities with screening, health education and referrals, delivered with qualified partners.

Read about Medical Outreach

School sport

Gombe State Principals' Cup

The Principals’ Cup brings the Unity Cup spirit into schools. It promotes healthy competition, teamwork, discipline and talent identification, moving from local government preliminaries through senatorial-zone stages to a state-level final.

Read about Principals' Cup

Initiatives beyond the five.

Basketball development, agricultural empowerment, peace advocacy and widows’ assistance are part of our record.
