Our Programmes

Our work, explained.

Five programme areas reflect the foundation’s focus on opportunity, learning, health, community participation and grassroots talent.

01 / Education

Educational Empowerment

Educational opportunity should not depend solely on a family’s ability to meet every schooling expense. This programme area focuses on financial assistance and scholarships for indigent learners at secondary and tertiary levels.

Read full programme →
02 / Skills

Vocational Training

Vocational training responds to the gap between ambition and usable skills. Through hands-on learning, participants can build technical capabilities for work, small enterprise and everyday economic independence.

Read full programme →
03 / Grassroots sport

Gombe South Unity Cup

The Unity Cup began in 2016 and has developed into a recurring football initiative across Gombe South. The foundation’s earlier website describes a model that discovers young talent through competition, develops players through follow-up camps and engagement, and seeks opportunities to promote them to a broader sporting audience.

Read full programme →
04 / Community health

Medical Outreach

For many households, even basic check-ups and preventive support are difficult to access. Medical outreach is intended to connect vulnerable communities with screening, health education and appropriate referrals through qualified partners.

Read full programme →
05 / School sport

Gombe State Principals’ Cup

The proposed Principals’ Cup is intended to promote healthy school competition, teamwork, discipline and grassroots talent identification. Planning documents outline a journey from local-government preliminaries through senatorial-zone stages to a state-level finale.

Read full programme →

Initiatives beyond the five.
