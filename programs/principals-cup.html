Gombe State Principals’ Cup

A proposed statewide boys’ public secondary school football competition designed to encourage school engagement and identify young football talent.

Request competition details ↗
Why it matters

A new stage for school football across Gombe State.

Our approach is about connecting possibility with practical action, in ways that respond to real community needs.

PROJECT BRIEF · 2026

Competition outline

Based on the foundation's October 2026 sponsorship documentation, the intended structure covers 11 local government areas with LGA preliminaries, senatorial zonal stages and a state final. No opening dates or online application route are announced here.

01

Statewide participation

The current project plan covers boys’ public secondary schools across all 11 LGAs of Gombe State.

02

Progressive competition

The proposed structure moves from LGA preliminaries to senatorial-zonal stages and a state finale.

03

Meaningful engagement

The competition is intended to encourage sportsmanship, school participation and community connection.
