# Project context: Adamu Yola Foundation website

## What this is
Website for the Adamu Yola Foundation (Gombe, Nigeria). Owner and builder: Oluwaseyi Ashiru. Repo: github.com/seyidev-ops/ayf. Live at https://adamuyolafoundation.org (GitHub Pages, branch `main`, root, `CNAME` present). Contact email on the site: adamuytfoundation@gmail.com.

## Brand and wording
- Approved tagline: "Education, Entrepreneurship and Youth Development". Share image line: "Opportunity begins with people. Education. Skills. Health. Sport."
- Logo: 2026 brand kit, a layered animated mark (arc, four figures, four heads) in `assets/brand/mark/`. It levitates and spins every 9 seconds. Keep wordmarks small enough to stay on one line in header and footer.
- Programme badges: 10th Anniversary Unity Cup badge and the Principals' Cup crest. No trace of the old logos or old Unity Cup mark may remain.
- Colours and fonts are tokens at the top of `assets/site.css` (navy, sky blue, pitch green; Bricolage Grotesque and Plus Jakarta Sans). Light and dark themes via `data-theme`, toggle key `ayf-theme`.

## Writing rules
- No instructional or internal statements on public pages. No double-dash sentences. Avoid repeating the same text across pages.
- Plain, warm, factual copy. Do not invent dates, scores or names. If a date is unknown, label the item by type (for example "Sport project").
- Navigation follows the approved sitemap: Home; About Us (Our Story, Mission, Vision & Values, Leadership & History); Programs & Projects (Educational Scholarships, Vocational Training, Gombe South Unity Cup, Medical Outreach, Gombe State Principals' Cup, Past Initiatives & Collaborations); News & Media (News and Announcements, Photo & Video Gallery); Get Involved (Partner/Sponsor, Volunteer, Support/Donate); Contact. Enquiries live inside Contact. Privacy, Terms and All Rights Reserved sit in the footer.

## How it is built
- Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS. Generated by `python3 tools/build.py` (data lives in it: `NAV`, `PROGRAMMES`, `NEWS`, `GALLERY`, `VIDEOS`). Never hand-edit the generated `.html` files; edit the script, rebuild, commit both.
- `V` in `tools/build.py` is the cache-bust string. Bump it on every build that changes CSS, JS or images.
- All links are relative so the site works under `/ayf/` and on the custom domain. `__PFX__` is replaced per page depth.
- Photos: webp in `assets/photos/` with a `-sm` thumbnail each. Gallery entries are `(category, path, title, meta, source, w, h)`. Filters open from a hash (`gallery.html#basketball`) and are handled at the end of `assets/site.js`.
- Programme pages can show an "In pictures" block (`pics`) linking to gallery filters, and a `badge`.
- Social image: `assets/og-image.jpg` (1200x630) for all pages. `assets/social-preview.png` (1280x640) is for the GitHub social preview, which can only be uploaded in repo Settings.

## Photo sourcing
Real photos only, from the foundation's Facebook (AdamuYolaFDN) and Instagram (adamuyolafdn) and from images the owner supplies. AI images only where no real photo exists. Public logged-out access reaches only a small part of the Facebook archive; see `PHOTO_ARCHIVE_STATUS.md`.

## Quality checks before every push
- Run the build, then check each page at 360, 768 and 1280 px: no horizontal overflow, no broken images, footer links on single lines, mobile drawer works, both themes readable.
- Show the owner a preview of big changes before deploying; small content updates the owner has requested may be pushed directly.
- Rebase on the remote before pushing (the owner sometimes edits on GitHub).

## Planned: football features
Fixtures, results and league tables for the Gombe South Unity Cup and the Gombe State Principals' Cup. Guidance for when this starts:
- Keep the site static. Store data in plain files (for example `data/unity-cup/<edition>/fixtures.json`, `results.json`, `teams.json`) and have `tools/build.py` render pages and tables from them, so updating a score means editing one data file and rebuilding.
- Compute tables (played, won, drawn, lost, goals for and against, goal difference, points) from results rather than typing them in. Confirm the points system and tie-break rules with the owner first.
- Likely pages under Programs: Fixtures and Results, Tables, Teams, and a per-edition archive (the 10th edition is the current anniversary one). Add them to `NAV` only after the owner approves the structure.
- Tables must stay readable on phones (horizontal scroll inside the table wrapper only, never the page), with sticky team column and light and dark theme support.
- Use only confirmed data supplied by the owner or published by the foundation. Never invent results.
