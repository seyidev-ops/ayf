Coming up · Grassroots football

Gombe South Unity Cup

The football tradition continues. Since 2016, the Unity Cup has brought communities together around competition, respect, player development and talent discovery.

Past editions have included post-tournament camping, peace campaigns and participation across Gombe South. The date of the forthcoming edition has not yet been confirmed for publication.
