What we do

Five programmes. One shared purpose.

From keeping learners in school to discovering football talent, each programme opens a door for someone in our communities.

All programmes
Grassroots sport

Gombe South Unity Cup

A grassroots tournament where football brings communities together and creates pathways for emerging players.

Education

Educational Scholarships

Helping students continue their education when financial barriers threaten their progress.

Skills

Vocational Training

Practical learning that strengthens livelihoods, earning ability and self-reliance.

Health

Medical Outreach

Community-centred efforts to bring basic healthcare services closer to people who need them.

School sport

Gombe State Principals' Cup

An inter-school platform designed to connect boys’ public secondary schools across all 11 local government areas of Gombe State.

The Gombe South Unity Cup

Since 2016 the Unity Cup has brought communities together around competition, respect, player development and talent discovery. Past editions have included post-tournament camping and peace campaigns across Gombe South.

  • DiscoverGrassroots players get a stage.
  • DevelopCamps, scouting and training.
  • PromotePathways to clubs and beyond.
  • UnitePeace and fair play first.

Our journey

A decade of community activity.

Documented milestones from the pitch, the classroom and the farm.

Full history
  1. 2016

    The Unity Cup kicks off

    Community football across Gombe South begins its journey.

  2. 2018

    The foundation is established

    Headquartered in Gombe with a focus on sports, education and entrepreneurship.

  3. 2020

    SportVote partnership

    The 5th edition expands visibility for grassroots players.

  4. 2021

    North-East Elite Basketball Camp

    Young athletes train with the Jordan Nwora Foundation.

  5. 2022

    18 players to an Abuja camp

    Unity Cup talent moves a step closer to the next level.

  6. 2023

    Live on Amana FM

    The 8th edition reaches listeners across the region.

  7. 2026

    All Stars FC crowned

    The 9th edition final is won in January.
